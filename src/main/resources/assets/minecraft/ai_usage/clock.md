---
id: minecraft:clock
targets: item:minecraft:clock
summary: tells the time of day
---
Shows the sun and moon position on the item. The `read` action gives the same answer as data, so you can plan for nightfall without looking at the sky. It is also the example of an action backed by a Java handler.

```actions
{"read": {"summary": "read the time of day", "params": []}}
```
